Brand launch

Society of Grownups

Launching a venture built for millennials.

Client
Society of Grownups, a MassMutual company
Role
Media agency of record
Outcome
Foot traffic, web activity and PR buzz

The starting point

In partnership with MassMutual and IDEO, UNCOVr was the media agency of record for the brand launch of this millennial focused venture.

What we did

We used the visual components of the design heavy campaign to connect with the younger target in geotargeted local and digital environments, with an authentic voice and a unique concept.

What happened

The campaign drove a tremendous amount of foot traffic and web activity, and helped elevate the PR buzz even more.
