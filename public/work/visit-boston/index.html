Tourism

Visit Boston

Showing visitors all the ways Boston is more than they expected.

Client
Visit Boston
Campaign
All Inclusive Boston, $2.5 million
Outcome
400% increase in web traffic

The starting point

Boston has a hip food scene, historic sites, cultural landmarks, and tech and healthcare hubs. Research showed it also has a perceptual racism problem, with a reputation as rude, whitewashed, traditional and aggressive. All Inclusive Boston, a $2.5 million tourism campaign for the city, set out to shift that narrative and tell the story of the people who live, work and create communities in Boston.

What we did

An overarching brand video and voiced :30 spots set the message, with the core spot voiced by Boston’s Poet Laureate, Porsha Olayiwola. Community print, hyper local digital and outdoor focused the impact, reaching people in their own language and in the media they respond to. Digital tied into the GBCVB’s Arrivalist tracking to keep the plan accountable. We bought as much media as possible from Massachusetts based partners, many hit hard by the pandemic, including The Bay State Banner, Bay Windows, WGBH/WBUR, Independent Newspaper Group, Dorchester Reporter, Sampan, El Mundo and El Planeta.

What happened

Our local partners, many of them 20+ year relationships, over delivered for the city. Outdoor delivered a 3:1 media value at launch. Some placements stayed up for months after the campaign ended, which conservatively pushes that to a $500k value, over 5:1. Radio delivered over $350k in media value on a spend of $150k. Digital partners added over 50 million in bonus inventory, an estimated value of about $400k.

Campaign results

  • 57.2Mimpressions
  • 68,000clicks
  • 1Msocial media views
  • 400%increase in web traffic to visitboston.com
  • 47%more foot traffic in measured locations for the exposed group versus the control group
  • +25%engagement rate on rich media and video
  • 70%video completion rate in drive markets
  • 95%+completion rate on OTT
