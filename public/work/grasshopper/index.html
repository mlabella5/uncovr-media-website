Market testing

Grasshopper

From 4 local markets to a national brand.

Client
Grasshopper, now part of LogMeIn
Channel
Network radio
Outcome
3x CPM savings

The starting point

Grasshopper began with market tests in 4 local markets.

What we did

UNCOVr’s market testing tool allowed a methodical expansion from those 4 local markets to a national campaign. National activity was planned to deliver similar GRP levels in our local test markets to ensure testing was fairly measured.

What happened

The national campaign delivered 3x CPM savings and served as a foundation for their marketing until the company’s acquisition by LogMeIn.
