Radio

Kabbage

Testing radio with no creative in hand.

Client
Kabbage
Channel
Radio
Outcome
National expansion

The starting point

Kabbage wanted to test radio but had no creative.

What we did

Working with our media partners, UNCOVr made Kabbage the first radio advertiser to feature Lori Greiner of Shark Tank. The talent was negotiated as added value within the media package. Additional vertical personalities added to the campaign included Jon Taffer of Bar Rescue, Tabatha Coffey of Tabatha Takes Over and NFL star and entrepreneur Tiki Barber.

What happened

The test expanded into a national rollout, with 3 years of additional testing. Kabbage was then acquired by American Express for $850 million. Its platform now powers American Express Business Blueprint, the small business loans offered to American Express business cardholders.
