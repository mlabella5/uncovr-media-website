Questions

Frequently asked questions

What is UNCOVr?

A research driven media agency built around collaboration and fresh thinking. We help national and local brands connect with their best prospects.

Who will I work with?

The senior people who set up the relationship are the same people doing the work. We started UNCOVr to get close to our clients’ businesses and away from agency red tape.

What kinds of media do you plan?

Audio, video, digital, print, outdoor and experiential. If the right opportunity does not exist yet, we have been known to create one from scratch.

How does research fit in?

We use tools we helped create at the large agencies we came from, along with some new ones. Upfront research and expert analysis come before the media investment, so the plan rests on numbers.

Where are you based?

Our mailing address is in Boston, and our partners are located in Boston, New York, Denver and San Francisco. We were virtual before virtual was cool, and we prefer to come to you.

How do I start?

Reach out by email or phone. We are always accessible and happy to learn more about your business.
