UNCOVr media has been active since 2014. Here are some brief snapshots of what makes our agency unique. Our client’s success is our best accolade.

Small Business Connections

Kabbage

Radio

We wanted to test radio but had no creative. Working with our media partners, we were the first radio advertiser to feature a shark from Shark Tank, Lori Greiner, in our advertising – and the talent and record were negotiated as added value within the media package! This relationship expanded into a 3-year partnership.

Test and Grow

Grasshopper (now part of LogMeIn)

Radio · National

UNCOVr’s market testing tool allowed a methodological expansion from 4 local markets to a national campaign with 3x the CPM savings. At the peak of the national campaigns, UNCOVr fluctuated between the #1 and #2 ranking of the largest buyers of network radio in Boston.

Hyperfocused Millennial Connections

Society of Grownups (a MassMutual Company)

Digital · Brand Launch

In partnership with MassMutual and IDEO, UNCOVr media was the media Agency of Record for the brand launch of the millennial-focused venture. We leveraged the visual components of this design-heavy campaign to connect with the younger target in geotargeted local and digital environments. With an authentic voice and unique concept, this campaign drove a tremendous amount of foot traffic and web activity and helped elevate the PR buzz even more.

Always Focused on Our Clients

Atrius Health

Outdoor · Community

An active partner since the beginning, knowing the challenges our partners at Atrius Healthcare in Boston were having with COVID-19, we wanted to find a way for them to connect with the frontline workers keeping the city going. Leveraging our partnerships with the outdoor media community, we secured over $150K in free media to let Boston know we’re here for them. Very proud of this campaign and the impact it continues to have.
